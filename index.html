Pocket Putt

Your phone is the putter grip. Hold it in both hands, screen up, look down at the ball and swing like a real putt.

Look down at the ball and tap Set: you're lined up on the hole and the putter rests on the green. The putter can only hit the ball once you're Set. Lift the phone to look at the hole any time, then look back down and tap Set again.

Play with friends

Up to 4 players, each on their own phone. You take turns in order, and watch from behind the line while the others putt.

Game code

Sends a link by text, WhatsApp or anything else. Or friends can open pocket-putt.netlify.app, tap Play with friends and enter the code.

    Talk while you play. Skip it if you're all in the same room. Earbuds stop echo.
